# Vehicle History Blockchain

A comprehensive vehicle history tracking system that creates tamper-proof records of maintenance, accidents, ownership transfers, and mileage verification to combat fraud and increase transparency in the used car market.

## Overview

This blockchain-based platform maintains immutable records of every significant event in a vehicle's lifecycle, from manufacturing to resale. By providing complete transparency, we prevent title washing, odometer fraud, and other deceptive practices that cost consumers billions annually.

## Core Features

### Comprehensive Vehicle Registry
- VIN-based unique identification system
- Manufacturer specifications and original sale data
- Complete lifecycle tracking from factory to current owner

### Service History Documentation
- Every service appointment with detailed work performed
- Parts replaced with OEM vs aftermarket tracking
- Mechanic certification verification
- Maintenance schedule compliance monitoring

### Accident and Claims Recording
- Insurance claim documentation
- Damage extent and location mapping
- Repair quality verification
- Total loss determinations and salvage status

### Ownership Transfer Tracking
- Complete title information and history
- Sale prices for market value analysis
- Lien holder details and payoff status
- State DMV integration for title transfers

### Odometer Verification
- Readings recorded at every service and inspection
- Anomaly detection algorithms to flag rollback fraud
- Mileage progression validation
- Alert system for suspicious patterns

### Recall and Compliance Management
- Active recall tracking by VIN
- Completion status of recall remediation
- Outstanding recall notifications to owners
- Safety compliance history

### Modification Registry
- Aftermarket parts and upgrades
- Performance modifications
- Impact on insurance and valuation
- Restoration documentation for classic vehicles

## Impact

The used car market suffers from severe information asymmetry. Buyers often purchase vehicles with:
- Rolled-back odometers (450,000+ cases annually)
- Hidden accident damage
- Undisclosed flood or hail damage
- Salvage titles washed to clean
- Incomplete service records

This platform levels the playing field, creating a trustworthy marketplace where honest sellers command premium prices and buyers make confident decisions.

## Getting Started

### Vehicle Owners
1. Register your vehicle with VIN
2. Upload existing service records
3. Authorize mechanics to update records
4. Receive maintenance and recall alerts
5. Generate reports for potential buyers

### Service Providers
1. Register as certified mechanic/shop
2. Request access to service vehicles
3. Document all work performed
4. Upload parts and labor details
5. Build reputation through quality work

### Buyers
1. Request vehicle history report by VIN
2. Review complete service and accident history
3. Verify odometer readings
4. Check recall compliance
5. Make informed purchase decision
